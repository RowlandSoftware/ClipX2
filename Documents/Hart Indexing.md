# **Alphabetisation - Hart's Rules**

**Sort order for Indexes**


## **BS ISO 999: 1996**


- **Word-by-word for general indexes in Britain**

- **Letter-by-letter used by encyclopedias, gazetteers, some dictionaries in Britain**

- **More common in US**


## **Word-by-Word**


- **Up to first space then begins again: *high water* before *highball***

- ***Hyphens as spaces: hyphenated words as if open *unless* first element not a word in own right e.g. de-emphasis, proto-language, iso-osmatic.**

## ***Letter-by-Letter**

## ***Proceeds across spaces: separated and hyphenated words treated as one word**


## ***Both Systems**

## ***Ignore apostrophes, diacritics, and accents**

## ***Ignores parentheses: *high (light-headed)* treated as *high.***

## ***Proceeds until comma, indicates inverted order, e.g. *High, J.* treated as *High*, unless more than one:**
